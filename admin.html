The Sheet ID is the long string in your Google Sheets URL between /d/ and /edit

📋 Setup Guide
1
Create a Google Sheet
Go to sheets.new to create a new spreadsheet. Name it "Sajawal Portfolio".
2
Add these column headers in Row 1
title  url  thumb  format  tag  duration  views  private

Each column in order A → H. Rename the sheet tab at the bottom to Videos.
3
Make the sheet publicly readable
Click Share → Change access to "Anyone with the link" → Role: Viewer. This lets the portfolio read your video list without login.
4
Copy the Sheet ID
Your sheet URL looks like:
https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1BxiMVs0XRA5nFMdKvBdBZjgmUUqptlbs74OgVE2upms/edit
Copy the highlighted part and paste it above.
5
For Google Drive videos
Upload your video to Google Drive → Right-click → Share → "Anyone with the link" → Copy link. Paste that link when adding a video here. It will embed directly.
6
For thumbnails
Upload thumbnail to Google Drive → Share → "Anyone with the link" → Copy link → Change the URL from drive.google.com/file/d/ID/view to drive.google.com/uc?id=ID. Or use any direct image URL (Imgur, etc).
